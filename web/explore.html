Can a backtest be trusted?

Every number on this page comes from the ghostdesk studies: about 47,700 company reports and ten years of S&P 500 prices. Change the settings and see how the answer moves. The page shows only statistics, never prices or trades.

Switch off one guard

The study has guards against common backtest mistakes. Each button runs the same study (2016–2021, before the test years) with one guard switched off. The blue bar is the honest result; the red bar is the result with the mistake.

The signal, period by period

Stocks are split into five groups by how much their newest report changed. The desk buys group 5 (changed least) and sells group 1 (changed most). A rising chart means the signal worked in that period.
