Nightly predictions · S&P 500 · scored forward

Every model, every stock, every night

After each close the frozen models predict the next day for all 500 stocks. The predictions go into an append-only ledger before the next open, and each one is scored once its two next opens exist. No orders are sent: order execution is off.
