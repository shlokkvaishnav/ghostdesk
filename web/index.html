Lazy reports, traded every month

The desk reads every 10-K and 10-Q. It buys the companies whose new report is most like the last one, and sells short the companies that rewrote theirs. Fake money, real prices.

    Each rebalance is split into small orders over the first hour, in step with the market's usual volume. In our tests this beat a trained RL agent.
