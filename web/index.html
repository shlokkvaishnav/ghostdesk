ghostdesk How it is built
Nightly predictions · S&P 500 · scored forward

Every model, every stock, every night

After each close the frozen models predict the next day for about 500 stocks. The predictions go into an append-only ledger before the next open, and each one is scored once its two next opens exist. No orders are sent: order execution is off.

How it works

A research platform for next-day stock prediction. Nothing here sends an order.

1 · DATAPoint-in-time S&P 500

Survivorship-free: each day uses the index members of that day, including the ones later removed, and only data known before the close.

2 · MODELSML and deep-learning models

Baselines, ridge, LightGBM, a transformer and two pretrained forecasters (Chronos-2, TimesFM 2.5) predict each stock's next-day residual return: its move net of market and sector.

3 · PORTFOLIORisk-constrained optimizer

A cvxpy optimizer turns the scores into a long-short book with limits on position size, sector and beta exposure, and a penalty on trading costs.

4 · PAPER TRADINGOrder execution is off

Every night the predictions and the target book go into an append-only ledger before the next open and are scored forward. No order is sent.

Historical validation, 2021–2022
